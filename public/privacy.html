🎨 Magic Paint Castle

Google Play Families & COPPA Compliant

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

👶 Summary for Parents and Guardians Magic Paint Castle is designed as a safe, wholesome creative environment for kids of all ages. We do not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information. No accounts, logins, or tracking of any kind are used.

1. Introduction

Magic Paint Castle ("we", "our", or "the app") is a kids' digital painting and coloring application. We place the highest priority on protecting children's privacy and providing a safe, advertisement-free, creative environment. This Privacy Policy describes our policies regarding data collection, usage, and protection in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Google Play Designed for Families policy.

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

We strictly follow COPPA rules and guidelines:

  • No Personal Information Collected: We do NOT knowingly collect, request, or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).
  • No Account Registration: Children and parents can enjoy all features without registering, logging in, or providing an email address, name, or phone number.
  • No Location Data: The app does not request, track, or access precise or approximate GPS location.
  • No Behavioral Tracking: We do not track kids across websites or apps.

3. How Drawings & Artworks Are Stored

All artwork, drawings, stamps, and coloring pages are saved locally on your device (using the browser's LocalStorage / IndexedDB storage mechanism). Your child's drawings are stored privately on your phone, tablet, or computer and are never transmitted to our servers or accessible by anyone else.

Users can view, rename, or permanently delete any saved artwork at any time via the "Open / My Drawings" art book menu within the app.

4. Third-Party Advertising and Analytics

  • No Ads: Magic Paint Castle contains zero third-party commercial advertisements, banner ads, or pop-up ads.
  • No Advertising SDKs: We do not include any ad-targeting or behavioral profiling software development kits (SDKs).
  • No Tracking Cookies: The app does not use third-party tracking cookies.

5. Optional Magic Art Companion (AI Feedback)

The app includes an optional "Magic Companion" feature (Bella the Bunny, Ollie the Owl, Dexter the Dino) designed to encourage children by giving uplifting, positive feedback on their drawings (e.g., "I love the bright colors in your picture!").

  • When a user taps "Ask Companion", a temporary snapshot of the current canvas drawing is sent securely over encrypted HTTPS to generate a child-friendly motivational response.
  • This drawing snapshot is processed in-memory solely to generate the short cheer message and is discarded immediately after. It is never used to identify the user, sold, or shared.

6. Device Permissions

The app may prompt for the following device permissions only when explicitly triggered by the user:

  • File Storage / Photo Library: Only requested when the user taps "Save Drawing" to save their completed artwork PNG to their device's photo gallery or downloads folder.
  • Audio Output: Uses device speakers to play playful kid-friendly brush sound effects and optional cheerful mascot voiceovers. No microphone input is ever recorded.

7. Data Security

Because we do not collect personal user data or host user accounts on remote databases, there is no risk of your child's personal data being leaked from our servers. All network transmissions (such as loading the app or optional AI encouragement) use industry-standard HTTPS encryption.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect improvements to our app or changes in relevant regulations. Any updates will be posted directly on this page with a revised "Effective Date".
